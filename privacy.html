Investsure Financial Inc. (“Investsure”, “we”, “us”) is a mortgage brokerage licensed in Manitoba and Alberta. We collect personal information in order to arrange mortgage financing and related insurance, and we handle it under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

Depending on how far your enquiry goes, we may collect:

  • Contact information — your name, telephone number, email address and what you tell us when you call, email or message us.
  • Financial information — income, employment, assets, debts, down payment source and credit history, when you proceed with an application.
  • Identification — government-issued identification, as lenders and the law require.
  • Property information — details of the property being purchased, refinanced or renewed.
  • Website data — standard technical information your browser sends, and analytics data if analytics are in use. analytics to confirm

Why we collect it

  • To answer your enquiry and tell you what financing may be available.
  • To prepare, submit and administer a mortgage application on your behalf.
  • To obtain a credit report, with your consent.
  • To offer mortgage-related insurance where you ask us to.
  • To meet our obligations under provincial mortgage brokering legislation and federal anti-money-laundering rules.
  • To contact you about your renewal, unless you tell us not to.

Your consent

We collect, use and disclose your personal information only with your consent, except where the law permits or requires otherwise. When you call, email or message us, you consent to us contacting you about that enquiry. A credit check is never run on the strength of an enquiry — it happens only later, with your separate, explicit consent.

You may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal and contractual limits, by writing to us. If you withdraw consent during an application, we may no longer be able to arrange financing for you.

Who we share it with

To arrange financing we may disclose your information to:

  • lenders and mortgage default insurers considering your application;
  • credit reporting agencies, with your consent;
  • insurers, where you have asked for insurance;
  • lawyers, notaries and appraisers involved in the transaction;
  • service providers who host our systems or process documents on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations;
  • regulators, where required by law, including the Manitoba Financial Services Agency and the Real Estate Council of Alberta.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Where it is stored

Some of our service providers may store or process information outside Canada, in which case it may be accessible to the courts and authorities of that country. list of providers and countries to confirm

How we protect it

We keep personal information in access-controlled systems, limit access to the people who need it to do their work, and require confidentiality from our staff and contractors. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive documents by ordinary email — your broker will give you a secure way to upload them.

How long we keep it

We retain records for as long as provincial mortgage brokering legislation and federal law require, and then securely destroy them. retention period to confirm

Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for a copy, and ask us to correct anything inaccurate. We will respond within the time PIPEDA allows. We may ask you to verify your identity first.
