Insurance

Insurance, in plain words.

We arrange insurance for clients across Canada. It closes the mortgage if you die, so the family is not left paying it. It pays you an income if illness stops you working. It covers the medical bills when you travel, and the bills for your parents while they are staying with you here.

To start working with us on insurance you do not have to arrange your mortgage with us. These are two separate processes.

If you would like advice on insurance, or you already have a policy and want to find out whether we can offer you better terms, write to us or give us a call. We are always glad to answer your questions.

Ask us about coverage

Life insurance

Your family keeps the home.

A mortgage lasts twenty-five years. If you die before it is paid off, someone in your family has to carry on paying it. Life insurance closes the loan instead, and the house stays with them.

What it does

The mortgage closes, the house stays

Say there is $400,000 left on your mortgage. If you die, the insurer sends your family $400,000. The loan is closed that month, nobody comes to them about payments, and nobody has to put the house up for sale in a hurry.

The amount

You choose how much

Take what you still owe on the house, then add two or three years of your income so the family is not watching every dollar while they find their feet. That is the number. We work it out with you and show you what it costs a month.

The price

Your price never goes up

Sign at thirty-five and you keep paying the thirty-five-year-old price. At forty-five, at fifty-five, after a heart attack, after a cancer scare — the insurer cannot raise it and cannot cancel you.

It is yours

It stays with you

The policy is attached to you, not to this house and not to this loan. Sell, move to another city, buy somewhere bigger, change lender — it keeps running and you never have to apply again.

Timing

The earlier you take it, the less it costs.

The price depends on two things: your age and your health. Both of them move in one direction only. Waiting does not make the decision any easier — it makes it more expensive, and in some cases it takes the choice away from you altogether.

Age

Every year adds to the price

Ask us what your policy costs today, and what the same policy will cost in five years. We will show you both numbers side by side. For most people that comparison is what makes the case for taking the policy now instead of in five years.

Health

Illness closes the door

Right now every health question on the form gets a no from you, and that is the cheapest you will ever be. One blood test later the same insurer can say no to you, or say yes at a much higher price, or say yes but leave out the one illness you were worried about.

What you keep

Today's price, locked in

Approval fixes the price for the whole term. You keep today's age and today's health for the next ten, twenty or thirty years.

The rule

Took the mortgage? Take the coverage

Sign for the debt and cover it in the same month, while you are already doing paperwork. Waiting for a better moment is how families end up carrying a loan of several hundred thousand dollars with nothing standing behind it.

Get a price today

Asking us costs nothing and commits you to nothing. But the price you are quoted a year from now will not be a better one.

If you cannot work

Most claims are not about death.

They are about a year without income. The mortgage, the car and the groceries carry on arriving while you get better.

Disability

Money every month while you are off work

You break a leg badly, or you are told you need six months of treatment. From then on the money arrives every month until you are back at work. We set the amount so it covers the mortgage and the bills, and you choose how soon after you stop working it starts.

Critical illness

One payment after a serious diagnosis

You are diagnosed with cancer. A few weeks later one payment lands in your account, and nobody asks what you do with it. People use it for treatment that is not covered here, for a year away from work, or simply to pay the mortgage down so there is one less thing in the house.

Self-employed

If you work for yourself

The month you stop working is the month the money stops coming in. There is no employer plan behind you, no paid sick leave, and nobody who takes over the mortgage while you are not earning. For someone who works for themselves, this policy is the sick pay. There is nothing else.

First

Check what you already have

Some employers pay for part of this. We look at that before suggesting anything, so you do not buy the same thing twice.

Travel

Outside Canada you are not covered.

Your provincial health plan pays very little abroad, and often nothing at all. One night in an American hospital can cost more than the whole trip.

One trip

For the trip you have already booked

It covers a doctor, a hospital, an ambulance and the flight home if it comes to that. Buy it before you leave, because once you are abroad it is too late.

All year

If you fly several times a year

One annual plan covers every trip, up to a set number of days each time. For two or three trips a year it usually costs less than buying coverage each time.

Read this

The coverage on your credit card

Card coverage is usually short, and it often stops at a certain age or if you already have an illness. We read the wording with you before you rely on it.

Long stays

A winter in the south

A long stay needs a plan of its own, and your province also sets a limit on how long you can be away before your health card stops working. We check both before you go.

Visitors to Canada

Your parents are not in the health system here.

A visitor to Canada pays for a doctor or a hospital out of their own pocket. For an older parent staying several months that is a real risk, and for a Super Visa it is not optional at all — the application does not go through without the insurance.

Visitor coverage

While they are staying with you

Your mother is three weeks into her visit and ends up in the hospital. Without coverage that bill comes to you, and it is not a small one. With coverage the insurer pays it. You buy the policy for the length of the visit and extend it if they stay longer.

Super Visa

The policy the application asks for

The application needs medical coverage of at least $100,000, valid for one year from the day they arrive. We issue the policy and the confirmation letter, and we check the current requirement every time.

New here

The wait before your health card

In some provinces a new resident has to wait before provincial health care begins, and a short plan covers that gap. We will tell you whether your province has a wait at all.

Be honest

Illnesses they already have

Blood pressure, diabetes, an operation years ago — tell us about all of it at the start. These things change both the price and what is actually covered, and a policy bought by leaving something out is a policy that will not pay when it matters.

How it works

Four steps, and asking costs you nothing.

Step one

We look at what you have

We go through your work benefits and any older policies first, because often part of the job turns out to be done already.

Step two

We show the numbers

We show you what your family would be left paying and what it costs to cover that, on one page and before anything is signed.

Step three

We compare insurers

The same coverage costs a different amount at different companies, and they ask different health questions. We apply where you fit best.

Step four

We follow it through

Approval usually takes a few weeks, and sometimes a medical exam. We follow up with the insurer and keep you posted on where it stands.

Check what you already have

Later

We check it again every few years.

A child, a bigger house, a new job. The policy you bought five years ago can be too small now, or you may be paying for coverage you no longer need. When your mortgage comes up for renewal, we look at the insurance at the same time.
