What we actually do

Four stages, and we carry all four.

Most delays happen because documents arrive late. We front-load that work so the lender receives a complete file the first time.

Stage 01

We talk

Income, credit and down payment reviewed, and the realistic number named out loud.

Stage 02

We gather

Identification, a letter from your employer, pay stubs, tax slips, and ninety days of bank statements showing where your down payment came from — checked for the gaps a lender would catch later.

Stage 03

We place it

Your file goes to the lender whose rules already fit it. You receive a written commitment and full disclosure.

Stage 04

We close it

Conditions cleared, lawyer instructed, keys on closing day — and we are still here at renewal.

The panel

Where your file can go.

A brokerage does not have a single credit policy. We read the file first, then choose the lender whose rules already fit it.

The big banks

The Big Six banks

Best rates if you are on a salary, with clean credit and ordinary paperwork.

Provincial

Credit unions

Provincially regulated, so their qualifying rules can differ from the banks'. Often the answer on rentals and unusual income.

Broker only

Lenders without branches

You cannot walk into them — they lend only through brokers, and compete on the rate and on the penalty for breaking your mortgage early.

Alternative

Trust companies

For damaged credit, a business started recently, or a file a bank turned down over a single calculation.

Private capital

Private lenders

Short-term loans against the value of your home, taken with a written plan to move you back to a bank.

Insured

Insured mortgage programs

With less than 20% down your mortgage must be insured. CMHC, Sagen and Canada Guaranty do that, and run programs for newcomers and the self-employed.

Protection

Your family keeps the home.

A mortgage lasts twenty-five years, and a lot can happen in that time. Insurance closes the loan if you die, so the family is not left paying it, and it covers the payments if illness stops you working. We also arrange travel coverage, and medical coverage for parents who come to visit you in Canada.

Life

A fixed sum for your family

If you die, the insurer pays your family an agreed amount. They clear the mortgage and keep the home. The policy belongs to you, so it carries on if you move the mortgage somewhere else.

Income

If you get sick and cannot work

Disability insurance keeps the mortgage paid while you are not earning. Critical illness insurance pays you a lump sum after a serious diagnosis. Either way the home stays, and so do your savings.

Timing

The earlier you take it, the less it costs

The price depends on your age and your health. The same coverage costs more every year you wait, and after a diagnosis an insurer can refuse you. Took on the mortgage? Take the coverage with it.

Renewal

We check it again every few years

Your life changes: a child, a bigger home, a new job. The policy you bought five years ago can be too small now. When your mortgage comes up for renewal, we look at the insurance too.

All the insurance we arrange Check what you already have

Insurance is arranged under a separate insurance licence. It is not mortgage brokering. Whether you are covered depends on the insurer's approval and on the terms of your policy.
